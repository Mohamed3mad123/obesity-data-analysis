# <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Obesity Data Analysis</span>

## <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Project Overview</span>
This project explores the relationship between obesity and related health, lifestyle, and economic factors in **Egypt, Japan, and the United States** from 2000 to 2022.

The goal is to compare trends across the three countries and investigate how obesity prevalence changes alongside selected health and economic indicators.

## <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Objectives</span>
- Analyze obesity trends over time in the selected countries.
- Compare health, lifestyle, and economic indicators.
- Identify patterns and associations in the data.
- Communicate findings through clear and interactive visualizations.

## <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Countries and Time Period</span>
- Egypt
- Japan
- United States
- Planned period: 2000–2022

## <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Key Indicators</span>
The project plans to investigate the following indicators, subject to data availability and comparability:

- Adult obesity prevalence
- GDP per capita (PPP)
- Diabetes prevalence
- Insufficient physical activity
- Health expenditure per capita

## <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Tools and Technologies</span>
- **SQL:** Data querying and analysis
- **Python:** Data processing and analysis
- **Pandas & NumPy:** Data cleaning and manipulation
- **Tableau:** Exploratory visualization and data storytelling
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development

## <span style="color: #2E86C1;">Project Workflow</span>
1. Data collection and source verification
2. Data cleaning and preparation
3. Exploratory data analysis
4. SQL-based analysis
5. Visualization and dashboard development
6. Interpretation of findings and recommendations
